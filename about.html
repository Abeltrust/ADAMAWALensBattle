Our Story

About the Battle

The Adamawa Lens Battle is a photography competition that celebrates the art of seeing — bringing together photographers from across all 21 Local Government Areas to showcase their craft and compete for the community's vote.

The Adamawa Lens Battle was born from a simple idea: that photography is more than just pictures — it is a way of telling stories, preserving culture, and seeing the world through someone else's eyes. We wanted to create a platform where photographers from all 21 Local Government Areas of Adamawa State could showcase their work, compete fairly, and gain the recognition they deserve.

Now in its 2026 edition, the competition features twelve official contestants representing Local Government Areas across Adamawa State. Each contestant brings a unique perspective and a distinct voice to the craft, celebrating the rich cultural, human, and natural heritage of our communities.

Voting is open to everyone at ₦100 per vote. Supporters can vote as many times as they want by transferring to the official Sterling Bank account (0093415813 · Adamawa Lens Battle) and submitting their payment receipt via WhatsApp. Every vote counts towards propelling a photographer to the champion's podium.

The Journey

How It Works

01

Submission & Selection

Photographers from across the 21 Local Government Areas submit their most powerful cultural, portrait, and community work. The top 12 official contestants are selected to represent their LGA in the state-wide battle.

02

Community Voting

The gallery opens to the public. Supporters transfer ₦100 per vote to Sterling Bank · 0093415813 · Adamawa Lens Battle, then send their payment receipt via WhatsApp to cast verified votes. Multiple votes are welcomed!

03

Live Results

Votes are validated and tallied in real time. The leaderboard updates continuously, allowing supporters, photographers, and fans to track the rankings as the competition unfolds.

04

The Champion

When voting closes, the photographer with the most verified votes is crowned the official Adamawa Lens Battle champion, walking away with the ₦300,000 Cash Prize, the prestigious Trophy, and a Featured Documentary.
